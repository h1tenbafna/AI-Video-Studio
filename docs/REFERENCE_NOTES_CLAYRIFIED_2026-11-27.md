## Measured

## Patterns to reuse
1. Hook = a concrete image plus a gap in the first sentence. No intro card.
2. Shot variety: wide, close, macro, wide. Change roughly every 2-3 s.
3. Every line has a literal image (rule 8).
4. Parable structure: story (about 40 s), pivot ("in life..."), 3 modern examples, one imperative closing line.
5. Captions: bold yellow, two short lines, word-synced, key words emphasised, low-middle of frame (close to our style B).
6. Narration: short sentences, rhythm builds, natural pacing.
