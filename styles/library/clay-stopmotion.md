**Motion:** Stop-motion on twos (12 fps held to 30), per-frame boil jitter, mouth shapes swapped per frame, squash and stretch on hops.
**Light:** Soft warm key from above-left, cool fill, gentle rim; contact shadows on the floor.
**Camera:** Locked two-shot with cuts to speaker close-ups, a slow 5% push-in per shot, shallow depth of field.
**Good for:** A recurring series where characters explain or discuss any topic (science, money, habits, history, stories); brand mascots.
**Local cost:** About 0.5 s/frame at draft and 4.1 s/frame at 1080x1920 (measured, upper bound). Authoring a new episode is mostly the script and the prop plan.
