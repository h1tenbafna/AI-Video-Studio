**Look:** Off-white or deep-navy background, one strong accent, large numerals, thin axes, generous margins.
**Motion:** Bars and lines grow on easing curves; numbers count up; the key figure scales once and holds.
**Light:** None. Subtle shadow only under floating cards.
**Camera:** Static frame; layout shifts do the work.
**Good for:** Statistics, comparisons, timelines, business and news explainers.
**Local cost:** Low; Remotion concurrency 2.
