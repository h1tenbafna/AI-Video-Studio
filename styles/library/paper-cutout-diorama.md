routes: [blender, remotion]

**Look:** Layered card cutouts with visible paper fibre and rounded edges, stacked a few centimetres apart in a shallow box.
**Motion:** Layers drift at different speeds for parallax; pieces pop up on a stepped, slightly uneven rhythm like stop-motion.
**Light:** Warm key from above-left casting soft contact shadows between layers; gentle vignette.
**Camera:** Slow lateral slide with shallow depth of field on the active layer.
**Good for:** Stories, history, people and places, anything friendly and tactile.
**Local cost:** Moderate in Blender (shadow-heavy); Remotion fake-parallax is cheap.
