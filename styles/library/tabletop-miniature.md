**Look:** A small scene on a wooden table: matte clay-like models, tilt-shift blur at top and bottom of frame, small labels on tiny flags.
**Motion:** Pieces are placed by an unseen hand: lift, travel in an arc, settle with a tiny bounce.
**Light:** One warm lamp low to the side, long soft shadows, dark falloff at the table edge.
**Camera:** High three-quarter view that glides and gradually pulls back as the scene fills.
**Good for:** Processes, step-by-step plans, cause and effect, world-building overviews.
**Local cost:** Moderate to high in Blender; check VRAM before choosing Cycles.
