# Art Direction — "Sunlight, Turned Into Electricity"

**Design read:** premium science documentary. Calm authority, quiet wonder, one idea per beat.
Macro → micro → macro: the film starts in a landscape, shrinks to atoms, and grows back to a home.

## Palette — "Photon Noir"
| Role | Colour | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Sunlight / photon / energy | amber `#FFB347` → gold `#FFD27A` | The *only* warm light in the micro-world. Wherever energy is, warmth is. |
| Electron | cyan `#4FC3F7` | Always emissive, always moving with purpose. Never decorative. |
| Hole | magenta-rose `#FF6B8B` | Rendered as an empty glowing ring (a vacancy), never a solid ball. |
| Silicon world | graphite `#030407` → indigo `#0A0C1E` | Dark, deep, quiet. |
| Panel surface | silicon-nitride blue `#1B2A6B`, silver contacts | Physically based. |

## Signature device
**The single warm photon.** It appears once as a visible hero particle (scene 3, the climax of the
micro-journey) and is echoed only as warm light elsewhere. It is not reused as scaffolding.

## Scene distinctness (primary subject per scene)
1. Landscape — solar farm at sunrise (wide, warm, real-world scale)
2. Material — the panel's surface and its layered stack (macro, tactile, metallic)
3. Event — one photon, one bond breaking (intimate, extreme close, shallow DOF)
4. Mechanism — the p-n junction field as an engineered structure (cutaway, diagrammatic depth)
5. Flow — a circuit and an inverter (systemic, directional motion)
6. Place — a home and the grid in morning light (wide, human, resolved)

No two scenes share a primary subject.

## Type & labels
Inter / IBM Plex Sans, light weight, small caps tracking +6%, white at 85% opacity, hairline
leader lines (1 px), max one label cluster per scene, never more than 3 words per label.
Labels: `silicon wafer · 0.18 mm`, `electron` / `hole`, `n-type` / `p-type` / `electric field`,
`DC` → `AC`. No titles cards, no bullet text.

## Motion
Slow-in/slow-out camera (no linear moves), continuous forward momentum into the panel,
a breath at the junction (aha), then outward acceleration. Transitions are *motivated*:
light-bloom dissolve into the panel, focus-pull into the lattice, scale-match cuts.
Film grain 3–4%, gentle vignette, bloom only on emissive physics elements.

## Anti-patterns (enforced)
- random glowing particles with no physical meaning
- slide-like text cards, Ken Burns on stills
- electrons drawn travelling as holes in the wire; holes leaving the semiconductor
- more than one label cluster at once
