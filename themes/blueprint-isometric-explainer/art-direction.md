# Art direction: How the AI Compute Business Works
**Visual language:** a hand-drafted engineering sheet. Pale-blue drafting paper (#8FB8E0) with white grid, navy (#0B2A4A) outlines, flat isometric solids at a locked 35 degrees, one orange (#FF7A1A) accent used only for shapes, arrows and chips.
**Signature device:** the NUMBERED PART TAG. Every company name and key figure is a boxed tag with a circled index and a leader line that draws on, like a callout on a technical drawing. No plain floating captions.
**Type:** Space Grotesk, bold navy on pale tag fills (contrast about 7-12:1).
**Motion:** lines draw on before fills, parts slide along their axis and snap with a short overshoot, the sheet scrolls down the chain between beats, dashed "planned" outlines become solids as the narrator reaches them.
**Why this subject:** the topic is a supply chain of buildings and money flows, which is exactly what an isometric system drawing explains.
**Scene distinctness:** scene-1 chip tile + dial; scene-2 multi-building chain with scrolling camera; scene-3 one building + two chips; scene-4 giant number + tag bus + recap strip.
